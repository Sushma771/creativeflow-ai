WHY THIS EXISTS

The seam between chat and canvas

Most AI tools for creative teams are either a single chat box, or a blank node canvas you have to build yourself. HexCoded is building both at once — agentic chat and node-based workflows in Creative Studio. BriefFlow is the seam between them: an agent that reads a brief the way a creative director would, and lays it out as an editable pipeline before a single frame renders. Every step — shot, grade, voice, export — stays a real node you can rewrite, reorder, or hand to someone else.

Magnific pushes single-image quality. ImagineArt and OpenArt cover broad generation and template libraries. LTX Studio takes a script to a shot-by-shot film. BriefFlow's job is narrower and, I think, still missing: turn the plain-English ask that agencies and editors actually get from clients into the pipeline a node canvas already knows how to run.

This is a prototype: node previews are placeholder gradients, not real renders. The planning and rewriting is real, wired to Claude live. Swapping in HexCoded's own generation nodes — image, video, voice, grade — is the natural next step.
